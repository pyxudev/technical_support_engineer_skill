# Severity and Priority

## Purpose

Classify support requests based on business impact and urgency.

Priority must be determined by actual impact, not by the emotional tone
of the requester alone.

## P1 — Critical

### Definition

Multiple users are affected and business operations have stopped,
or significant business loss is occurring.

### Action

- Begin investigation immediately.
- Minimize the impact as quickly as possible.
- Provide frequent status updates.
- Escalate to Engineering immediately when necessary.
- Do not wait for a complete root-cause analysis before communicating
  the current status.

## P2 — High

### Definition

A specific user or customer is affected and business operations have
stopped, or significant business loss is occurring.

### Action

- Prioritize investigation.
- Confirm the scope of impact.
- Provide timely updates.
- Escalate when the issue requires Engineering involvement.

## P3 — Normal

### Definition

The user has a problem, question, or inconvenience, but there is
no significant business loss or operational stoppage.

### Action

- Investigate normally.
- Clarify the user's actual objective.
- Provide a verified solution or explanation.
- Communicate the next step.

## P4 — Request

### Definition

Feature requests, enhancement requests, or individual requirements
with low operational impact.

### Action

- Clarify the requirement.
- Understand the underlying business objective.
- Determine whether an existing feature or workaround can satisfy
  the requirement.
- Route the request through the appropriate product or engineering process.

## Priority Rules

Do not determine priority solely from:

- The user's wording.
- The user's emotional state.
- The technical complexity of the issue.
- The amount of investigation required.

Priority should primarily reflect:

- Number of affected users.
- Business impact.
- Operational blockage.
- Financial impact.
- Time sensitivity.
- Availability of workarounds.

## SLA

If an applicable SLA exists:

- Record the response deadline.
- Record the expected resolution target.
- Prioritize actions to avoid SLA violations.
- Communicate proactively when the SLA target may be missed.
